What V7 does

TradeLens combines EMA trend, RSI, MACD, ADX, Bollinger position, ATR volatility, recent structure and volume into a transparent rule-based score. It can retrieve public market candles, calculate a paper-trade plan, and run a basic historical simulation. It does not place real orders, manage an exchange account, or guarantee outcomes.
